# Defects carried by the register itself

Recorded, not fixed. Each is a defect **in the register**, not in a document.

1. **`ADM-001-VERS 004` is used for two different documents** — *Laboratory Temperature Control*
   (06/04/2025) and *Document and Record Control* (21/03/2024). Serial 002 is doubled the same way:
   *Documentation of Examination Procedure* (SOP-002) and *Laboratory Critical Results*
   (GUID-002) are different series, so that one is only a reading hazard. Serial **001** is a true
   collision.

   (GUID-003 row lists 001) and *Laboratory Quality Control Manual* (MAN-001). Different types, so
   permitted, but the register prints identical numbers with no type letter in the left column.

   (10/03/2024) and *Handling of biological spills* (29/05/2024). **True collision.**
5. **`BIO-008-VERS 003` covers two different documents** — *Aspartate Aminotransferase (ASAT)* and
   *Total Bilirubin (HumaStar 80)*. **True collision.**
6. **`HEM-008-VERS 003` covers two different documents** — *Coombs Control/Check Cells Preparation*
   and *Vein Blood Collection and Preparation of Hematology sample*. **True collision.** This is the
   collision the library already knew about; it is now recorded against the 2026 register.

   and *Stool Analysis for Parasites and/or their Eggs*. **True collision.**
8. **`SER-004-VERS 004` covers two different documents** — *Alere HIV Combo Ag-Ab* (18/03/2025) and
   *HBV Surface Antigen Rapid Testing* (20/03/2024). **True collision.**
