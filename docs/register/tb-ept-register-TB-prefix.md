## EQA for this section

The TB section is assessed by two different schemes and they are not interchangeable:

| Examination | Scheme type | What the provider assesses |
|---|---|---|
| Xpert MTB/RIF Ultra | **Proficiency testing panel** — prepared samples of known composition, tested blind and scored | The laboratory's whole examination process, from specimen handling to result |
| TB smear microscopy (ZN and auramine) | **Slide re-reading** — the laboratory sends its own stained slides and the results it reported; NRL reads them independently and compares | The laboratory's staining and its reading, against the provider's reference reading |

Both are governed by `RliDHL-ADM-SOP-025-VERS-001`, section 8 for panels and **section 8A** for slide
re-reading. A discrepancy on a re-read smear is a finding about a person's reading or the laboratory's
staining — the kind of finding internal quality control cannot produce, because the laboratory would be
comparing its own opinion with itself.
